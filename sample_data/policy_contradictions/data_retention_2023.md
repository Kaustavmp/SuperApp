# Data Retention Policy v1.0
**Effective Date:** January 15, 2023
**Last Updated:** March 1, 2023

## Purpose

This policy defines how long data is retained and when it must be deleted.

## Retention Periods

- **Customer personal data**: Retained for **3 years** after account closure
- **Transaction logs**: Retained for **5 years** per financial regulations
- **Employee records**: Retained for **7 years** after termination
- **Server access logs**: Retained for **90 days**
- **Marketing analytics**: Retained for **2 years**

## Data Deletion

All data past its retention period must be deleted within **30 days** of the retention deadline.

Deletion is performed automatically by the data lifecycle service. Manual deletion requests are processed within **5 business days**.

## Backup Policy

Backups are retained for **6 months** regardless of the source data's retention period. Deleted data may persist in backups until the backup rotation cycle completes.

## Exceptions

Data subject to active legal holds is exempt from automatic deletion. Legal holds are managed by the Legal department and override all retention schedules.

## Compliance

This policy complies with GDPR Article 5(1)(e) and CCPA Section 1798.105.
