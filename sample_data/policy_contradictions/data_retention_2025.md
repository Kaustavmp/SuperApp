# Data Retention Policy v2.0
**Effective Date:** June 1, 2025
**Last Updated:** June 1, 2025

## Purpose

This policy defines how long data is retained and when it must be deleted, in accordance with updated regulatory requirements.

## Retention Periods

- **Customer personal data**: Retained for **1 year** after account closure (reduced from previous policy to comply with updated GDPR guidance)
- **Transaction logs**: Retained for **7 years** per updated financial regulations
- **Employee records**: Retained for **7 years** after termination
- **Server access logs**: Retained for **1 year** (increased for security audit requirements)
- **Marketing analytics**: Retained for **6 months** (reduced per privacy-by-design initiative)

## Data Deletion

All data past its retention period must be deleted within **7 days** of the retention deadline (tightened from previous 30-day window).

Deletion is performed automatically by the data lifecycle service. Manual deletion requests are processed within **48 hours**.

## Backup Policy

Backups are retained for **30 days** with immediate purge of expired data. No deleted data may persist in any backup beyond this window.

## Exceptions

Data subject to active legal holds is exempt from automatic deletion. Legal holds are managed by the Compliance team. Additionally, data involved in ongoing security investigations is exempt.

## Subcontractor Data

All subcontractors processing data on behalf of the company must adhere to the same retention periods. Subcontractor compliance is audited quarterly.

## Compliance

This policy complies with GDPR Article 5(1)(e), CCPA Section 1798.105, and the Digital Services Act (DSA) Article 28.
